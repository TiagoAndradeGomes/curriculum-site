About Me

A Civil Engineer trained at Instituto Superior Técnico, I have been part of the Lisbon Drainage Master Plan (PGDL) team since July 2017 and have served as BIM Manager since January 2021.

I work on the owner's side, defining requirements, managing information, coordinating disciplines and integrating BIM with Geographic Information Systems (GIS). The initial contract value for the tunnels and associated works is €132.9 million excluding VAT.

My engineering background is complemented by postgraduate qualifications in BIM Coordination (ISEP) and Management (ISEG). I am a Board Member of buildingSMART Portugal and convenor of GT4 at the Portuguese Tunnelling and Underground Space Commission (CPT), a group within the Portuguese Geotechnical Society whose members contribute on a voluntary, unpaid basis.

BIM implementation, ISO 19650 and information management

  • Coordination across the owner, designers, supervision team and contractor.
  • Definition of Exchange Information Requirements (EIR), information needs, IFC deliverables, responsibilities, intellectual property rights and acceptance criteria.
  • Review and monitoring of the BIM Execution Plan (BEP), with quality assurance and quality control of geometry and information.

Common Data Environment — Autodesk Construction Cloud

  • Configuration of workflows, information states, permissions and submission rules.
  • Monitoring of submissions, revisions, approvals and audit trails against contractual requirements.

Technical coordination and training

  • Chairing BIM and technical coordination meetings, recording decisions, actions, owners and deadlines.
  • Practical support and training for multidisciplinary teams; liaison between technical teams, contract management and information systems.

BIM–GIS integration and asset information

  • Definition of the integration strategy between BIM models and the Council's Geographic Information Systems (GIS).
  • Structuring information to support asset management, maintenance and operation; development of a BIM–GIS proof of concept.

Public procurement, contract management and technical monitoring

  • Preparation of tender programmes and specifications, including BIM requirements; technical bid assessment and contributions to award reports.
  • Contract manager for the PGDL BIM support services contract: technical follow-up, deliverable review and contractual compliance.
  • Participation in other procurement procedures as procedure coordinator and tender jury member.
  • Technical monitoring of design and construction, investigations, tests and complementary studies, including LNEC scale models of vortex spillways and anti-pollution basins; technical review of detailed designs.
